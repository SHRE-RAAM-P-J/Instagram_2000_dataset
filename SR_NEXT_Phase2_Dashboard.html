Followers vs Engagement Rate
Scatter plot of 600 sampled influencers (ER ≤ 1.0 for readability). Dot size = Influence Score.
What the Scatter Tells Us
Inverse Trend: As follower count rises, engagement rate generally declines — a well-known influencer marketing pattern. Mega-accounts sacrifice intimacy for reach.
Micro-Influencer Advantage: Accounts with 5K–50K followers often show 3–10% ER — making them ideal for niche brand campaigns with high ROI.
Outliers: Several large accounts (500K+) maintain high ER — these are the most valuable influencers in the dataset.

Accounts flagged based on ER anomalies, follower-to-engagement mismatch, and posting frequency patterns
Risk Level Distribution
Detection Methodology
HIGH Risk: ER > 30% on accounts with >50K followers — statistically impossible organically, OR posting frequency >25/month with near-zero ER.
MEDIUM Risk: ER > 20% (suspicious for any account size), OR high frequency posting combined with below-average engagement.
LOW Risk: Normal engagement patterns consistent with organic growth and authentic audience interaction.
